All five public deity images are original AI-generated oil-painting-style illustrations, reviewed visually for recognizable basic iconography and hand/limb plausibility before publication. They are NOT copied or retrieved from a third-party website. They depict devotional, cultural artistic interpretations, not official scriptural proof of a unique correct form.

## Rights and limitations

The generated images are original outputs of an image generation process, not copies from reference photographs. No source attribution or public-domain status is claimed for other people's artwork. The artwork includes culturally informed invented detail and should not be used as a canonical religious authority. Keep this metadata with site source as part of editorial transparency.
